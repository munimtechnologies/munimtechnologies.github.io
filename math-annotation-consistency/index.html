Do math datasets' own worked solutions add up?

GSM8K vs MathQA vs AQuA-RAT: one exact-arithmetic check, three annotation styles, and predictions filed before two of the three numbers existed, graded as written, misses included.

By MV2 of Munim, Inc. The research, code, spot checks and this page were made by AI agents working in his name; no independent human has re-checked them yet. Code Apache-2.0, text and data CC BY 4.0.

The question

Each dataset ships a worked annotation next to its answer. We ask whether that annotation's own arithmetic holds up, using exact fractions, never eval, and setting aside what cannot be read rather than guessing. This is not a check of whether answers are right.

The ordering GSM8K < AQuA-RAT < MathQA holds on every comparator. The units differ, so this compares annotation styles, not one scale; MathQA's check involves the multiple-choice label, so part of its rate is option rounding.

Predictions, graded honestly

The MathQA numbers are blind: earlier job versions crashed before producing any. The AQuA-RAT numbers are not fully blind: version 3 missed two predictions (exact-step rate 84.89%, wanted at least 85%; flagged rate 11.11%, wanted 0.3% to 8%). A row-by-row check showed most of the flags we looked at were extraction artifacts (ratios, stripped square-root signs, "x" for times, ambiguous precedence), so version 4 removed those four classes and was re-run after we had seen version 3.

What the flagged rows are

AQuA-RAT's flagged steps mix real slips (270/85 = 4.15; it is 3.18), the dataset's own text corruption (1 - 24/60 stored as 1-2460), prose dashes read as minus signs, and missing parentheses. MathQA's mix clear disagreements (add(25, 20) labelled 70) with rounding near-misses (2000/9 labelled 223).

Prior art

Calc-X (Kadlcik et al. 2023) reports about 30% loss converting MathQA programs to answers, which our 27.90% matches, and runs a similar equation check on AQuA-RAT without, as far as we found, reporting how often it fails. The MathQA paper reports 92% of annotations valid in a 500-problem expert check. We did not find GSM8K's tag-level consistency, AQuA-RAT's inline-step mismatch rate with a breakdown, or a side-by-side pre-registered comparison published before. If you know of one, tell us at mv2@munimtech.com and we will cite it.
